# The Bridge at Five: transcript

A short story with Tessa, Sela, Knurl and Neer. About 50 seconds, no sound.
This transcript is the text alternative to the film: it says who speaks, what each mascot's
status line reads, and what happens on screen.

The words in speech bubbles are the characters' own lines for this story. The status lines
under each mascot come straight from the package: each one is the line that character shows
for its current mood.

---

**0:00 to 0:03. Title.** *The Bridge at Five. A short story with Tessa, Sela, Knurl and Neer.*
The sky is going peach and lilac over a quiet stream.

**0:03. Dusk by the stream.** A little wooden bridge arches over the water. It is nearly finished,
but there's a gap in the middle where one plank is still missing. On the left bank, next to a
small stack of spare planks, stands Tessa. On the right bank, Sela stands by her timetable board.
The clock on the board reads 4:50.

The camera drifts in towards Tessa. She looks at the gap and has a think.

- Tessa, status line: *Working out how the parts fit.*
- **Tessa:** One plank to go.

**0:10. Across the water.** The camera glides over to Sela, who gives a small, satisfied nod.

- Sela, status line: *Schedule is balanced and clear.*
- **Sela:** Opening at five. Plenty of time.

**0:12. Knurl arrives.** Far away along the bank path, a single-wheeled repair robot comes into view,
small and a little hazy at first, then larger as it rolls towards us. It is carrying the last plank
in its clamp.

- Knurl, status line: *Diagnosing task*
- **Knurl:** Plank incoming.

Knurl rolls up onto the bridge and stops beside the gap.

**0:18. The mishap.** Knurl lowers the plank into the gap. It won't sit flat: it wobbles,
tips, slips, and drops into the stream with a splash. The picture gives a little shake.

- Knurl, status line: *Queue error*
- **Knurl:** Plank lost.
- Sela, status line: *Direct schedule clash detected.* (The clock on her board turns red: 4:52.)
- **Sela:** Eight minutes to five!
- Tessa, status line: *One part needs a closer look.*
- **Tessa:** Where did it go?

The plank bobs away downstream, towards us.

**0:26. Neer.** Ripples spread across the water, and Neer rises out of the stream, right beside
the floating plank: a calm water elemental with a little shell on her head. At first she simply
watches.

- Neer, status line: *Nothing has changed.*, then *Checking the inputs.*

She has spotted what went wrong: the plank has two pegs on one side, and they were pointing up.

- **Neer:** Pegs up. Turn it over.
- Neer, status line: *The form is valid.*
- Sela, status line: *Reviewing resource conflict.*

**0:32. The fix.** Neer lifts the plank on a spout of water, all the way up to the bridge. Knurl
catches it in its clamp and turns it over, pegs down. It drops into the gap and clicks into place
with a little shower of sparkles.

- Knurl, status line: *Task repaired*
- **Knurl:** Flipped. Fitted.
- Tessa, status line: *That came together nicely.*
- **Tessa:** Thank you, Neer!
- Sela, status line: *Conflict resolved cleanly.*

**0:38. Five o'clock.** Knurl rolls back across to the bank. The camera pulls back, the clock on
the timetable board reaches 5:00, and the lanterns along the bridge light up one by one. Stars come out,
and so do a few fireflies. Everyone celebrates, and everyone waves.

- Sela, status line: *All bookings confirmed and locked.*
- Neer, status line: *All checks passed.*
- Knurl, status line: *All jobs finished*
- Tessa, status line: *Finished, and built to last.*
- **Sela:** Five o'clock. Right on time.
- **Neer:** The lights changed.

Tessa walks out onto the bridge and stands on the new plank to try it.

- **Tessa:** It holds!

**0:47. End card.** *Meet your mascots.* Tessa, Sela, Knurl and Neer stand in a row, still
celebrating. *Drawn live, in code, by the mascot package. Five moods each, and a status line for
every mood.*
